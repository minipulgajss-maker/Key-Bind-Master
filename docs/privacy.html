Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

KeyBind Master is built to keep your data on your PC. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. This page explains the little that does leave your PC and why.

What stays on your PC

Your shortcuts, settings, history, scripts, profiles, macros and Pro status are stored only in your Windows user folder. Pro status files are encrypted with Windows so they cannot be edited. We never see them.

What is sent, and why

DataWhy
A one-way fingerprint of your PC (a scrambled form of a Windows ID that cannot be turned back into the original)So invites and codes can only be used once per PC
A random key created by the app on your PCTo sign requests so nobody can pretend to be your PC
A scrambled form of your IP addressTo stop invite abuse from the same network. Your real IP address is not stored
App version and the time of the requestTo block old versions and replayed requests
Invite codes and code IDs you useTo give you Pro time and to turn off codes that were removed
Only if you send feedback on the website: your rating, message, the optional name you type and the language of the pageTo read your opinion and improve the app. The message is checked by an automatic filter for spam and abuse first. We do not store your email, and your IP address is only kept in a scrambled form for a short time to limit abuse

This is sent to the KeyBind server, which runs on Cloudflare, only when you use invites or codes and a few times a day to keep Pro time up to date.

What is never collected

  • Your email address, password or card details. Card details go only to Stripe
  • Your keystrokes. Shortcuts are detected on your PC and never sent anywhere
  • Your files, scripts, browsing, screenshots or clipboard
  • Which apps or games you have, except locally to show them in the app

Discord login and payments

  • Discord: if you sign in, we receive your Discord user ID and username so your codes and plan can be linked to your account. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your PC and is never shared.
  • Stripe: payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your card and billing details under its own privacy policy. We receive only the plan, status and dates of your payment, linked to your Discord ID. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

Other services

  • Updates: the app downloads new versions from GitHub. GitHub sees a normal download request from your IP address.
  • Connection check: Boost measures your ping by contacting public game and cloud servers. Only standard network packets are sent.
  • This website: it is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The Stripe payment page may set its own cookies needed for secure payment.

Cookies

This website and the app set no cookies and use no local tracking, so there is no cookie banner. The only exceptions are the cookies Stripe and Discord set on their own pages, which are needed for payment and sign-in security.

How long data is kept

Invite and code records are kept while they are useful for preventing abuse. Network counters delete themselves after at most 31 days.

Your choices

You can use KeyBind Master without invites or codes. Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder in your user profile removes everything stored on your PC. You can sign out of Discord in the app at any time, which unlinks the account from your PC. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. To ask for your PC's record on the KeyBind server to be deleted, contact us through the feedback page and include your invite code.

Children

Online features (Discord login, codes and payments) are for people aged 13 or older, or the minimum age in your country. People under 18 may buy only with the permission of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian can contact us through the support page to have a child's data deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
