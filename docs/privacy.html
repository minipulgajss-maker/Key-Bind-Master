Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

KeyBind Master is built to keep your data on your PC. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. This page explains the little that does leave your PC and why.

What stays on your PC

Your shortcuts, settings, history, scripts, profiles, macros and Pro status are stored only in your Windows user folder. Pro status files are encrypted with Windows so they cannot be edited. We never see them.

What is sent, and why

DataWhy
A one-way fingerprint of your PC (a scrambled form of a Windows ID that cannot be turned back into the original)So invites and codes can only be used once per PC
A random key created by the app on your PCTo sign requests so nobody can pretend to be your PC
A scrambled form of your IP addressTo stop invite abuse from the same network. Your real IP address is not stored
App version and the time of the requestTo block old versions and replayed requests
Invite codes and code IDs you useTo give you Pro time and to turn off codes that were removed

This is sent to the KeyBind server, which runs on Cloudflare, only when you use invites or codes and a few times a day to keep Pro time up to date.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address or any account details
  • Your keystrokes. Shortcuts are detected on your PC and never sent anywhere
  • Your files, scripts, browsing, screenshots or clipboard
  • Which apps or games you have, except locally to show them in the app

Other services

  • Updates: the app downloads new versions from GitHub. GitHub sees a normal download request from your IP address.
  • Connection check: Boost measures your ping by contacting public game and cloud servers. Only standard network packets are sent.
  • This website: it is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

How long data is kept

Invite and code records are kept while they are useful for preventing abuse. Network counters delete themselves after at most 31 days.

Your choices

You can use KeyBind Master without invites or codes. Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder in your user profile removes everything stored on your PC. To ask for your PC's record on the KeyBind server to be deleted, contact us through the support page and include your invite code.

Children

KeyBind Master does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
